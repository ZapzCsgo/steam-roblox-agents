Audience and policy constraints (they weigh on F5, which is rule-based, and on F10):

- The core audience is 9 to 17 years old. Drugs, sexual content, explicit gambling and
  realistic firearms are disqualifying or need a full re-theme.
- Mandatory voice chat is a problem: voice access on Roblox is restricted by age checks.
- Roblox removes a game when the rights holder files a claim. Literal copies with reused
  assets (E.R.P.O., CLIFF) were taken down; adaptations that translate a genre into Roblox
  culture (Forsaken, Dandy's World, Blair) lasted.

Lessons from 2020 to 2026 cases (use them as priors, not as rules):

- Every strong Roblox success was co-op or party play; solo games did not transfer.
- The Steam player peak alone predicts nothing (Palworld and Schedule I failed, Chained
  Together succeeded with a far lower peak).
- A late clone can still win in a durable genre (Supermarket Simulator about 7 months after
  the Steam peak), while hype concepts without a host genre fade within weeks.
- A viral concept can leak to another free platform instead of Roblox (Fall Guys went to
  Stumble Guys on mobile because obbies already covered the genre on Roblox).
